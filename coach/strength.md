# Strength training

Read by Claude when planning and analysing strength sessions, and by the dashboard: the phase table below sets the
targets and suggestions on the Progress → Strength page and on each gym session. Keep the table's layout (one row per
phase, the code in the first column) when you edit numbers; the text sections are free to change.

## Weight lifting phases

Guide for sessions, load and reps per phase (Friel, Table 13.3). Load is a percentage of your one-rep max (1RM); the
dashboard estimates your 1RM per exercise from your logged sets.

| Code | Phase | Period | Total sessions | Sessions per week | Load (% 1RM) | Sets per session | Reps per set | Recovery (min) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AA | Anatomical adaptation | Prep | 4–6 | 2–3 | 40–60 | 3–5 | 15–20 | 1–1.5 |
| MT | Max transition and ballistic | Prep | 4–6 | 2–3 | 70–80 | 3–5 | 8–12 | 2–3 |
| MS | Max strength and ballistic | Base 1 | 8–12 | 2–3 | 85–95 | 3–4 | 3–6 | 3–4 |
| SM | Strength maintenance and ballistic | Base 2, Base 3, Build 1, Build 2, Peak | indefinite | 1 | 60, 85 | 2 | 12, 6 | 1–2 |

Strength maintenance is 2 sets: one of 12 reps at 60 % and one of 6 reps at 85 % of 1RM.

## Exercises

The exercises in your Strength log (Notion). Add a line when you add an exercise.
Load is always the total weight you move: two 5 kg dumbbells = 10 kg; for a barbell, the bar plus plates.

| Exercise | Trains | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Double leg squat | hips, knees, quads, glutes | main lift; barbell or goblet |
| Leg press | quads, glutes | heavier load than the squat with less technique demand |
| Single leg squat | balance, hip and knee control | split squat or Bulgarian split squat; log both dumbbells together |
| Power step-up | single-leg power for running and cycling | drive up fast, lower slowly; log both dumbbells together |
| Leg curl | hamstrings | balances the quad work |
| Core | core stability | log the exercise in Reps, e.g. "plank 3×40s" |

## Principles

- Raise the load only when all sets reach the top of the rep range with good form; go up about 5 % (2.5 kg on most lifts).
- In MS, keep 1–2 reps in reserve; never train to complete failure to prevent injuries.
- Weights sessions in prep and early base count as hard days: at least 2 days between them, and a recovery day after.
- Do the gym session before an endurance session on the same day, or on another day.
