# Coaching principles

General principles for planning and analysis. If `coach/principles.local.md` exists (your own fuller notes, kept out of git), read that as well.

- **Least effective dose.** Do the least training that still brings steady progress, timed and specific to the goal.
- **Moderation.** Easy days easy, hard days hard but recoverable within about 48 hours. Weekly load rises at most
  ~10 % at a time.
- **Consistency.** A routine that stays roughly the same week to week beats big swings; rest is part of it.
- **Recovery.** Fitness is built while resting; sleep comes first.
- **Plan, but adapt.** Follow the plan, and adjust it to how the athlete responds.
- **Peak for A races.** Taper only for the most important races; rest a few days before B races.
- **Work on limiters.** Spend time on what holds the athlete back, not only on strengths.
- **Specificity.** The closer the goal race, the more training should look like the race.
- **Reversibility and individuality.** Fitness fades without training, and every athlete responds differently.
- **Duration over distance.** Plan workouts in time; intensity is tied to time.
- **Load = volume × intensity.** With little time available, add intensity rather than leaving the load too low.
- **Listen to the body.** Keep a log of how sessions felt, and keep it fun.
